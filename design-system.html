Rules

What to keep, what to kill.

Do

  • Lead with product evidence.
  • Use exact coverage language.
  • Keep the neutral palette disciplined.
  • Make stale data obvious.

Don't

  • Add SaaS blobs, mascot energy, or generic gradients.
  • Use status colors as decoration.
  • Introduce new font families.
  • Write CTAs that sound like committee soup.
